How to Set Up Your Zoom Name&Link

📅 June 2026 👤 TalkColleges Team

When you sign up as a TalkColleges advisor, we ask for your personal Zoom link so that students can join your sessions directly. This way, we can give more freedom to the advisor as to managing their own schedule.

What is a Personal Zoom Link?

Zoom gives every account a Personal Meeting Room — a permanent virtual space with a fixed link that never changes. Think of it like a permanent address for your video calls. When a student books a session with you, we'll send them this link in their confirmation email so they can join at the scheduled time.

Free account is fine. You don't need a paid Zoom plan to use TalkColleges. A free Zoom account gives you everything you need.

Step 1 — Go to Zoom

Go to zoom.us and sign in with a personal account.

Use an account you don't use often. We ask all advisors to change their Zoom display name to protect their identity during sessions. You should use a Google/Zoom account that you rarely use — we don't want your boss or professor seeing that your display name is "john-harvard'30-economics" on your next call.

Step 2 — Changing your display name

Once you have signed in, click on your profile picture on the upper right

Click "My account"

Click "Edit"

For the First Name and Display Name fields, please enter the name we sent in your acceptance email. Then hit "save".

Step 3 — Getting your meeting link

This is the meeting link that will be sent to students. Make sure which account it is under.

Scroll down to the Meeting section and click the eye icon.

Copy the entire zoom link.

Paste it in the Google Form

Last Step — Enabling waiting room

Make sure your Personal Meeting Room has a waiting room enabled so you can admit students at the right time. It is on by default in zoom. But if you want to double-check, go to Settings → search "waiting room" and toggle on Enable waiting Room.

What Happens Next?

Once you submit your Zoom link to TalkColleges, we store it securely on your advisor profile. When a student books a session with you and completes payment, we automatically send them your Zoom link via email — you don't have to do anything extra.

On the day of the session, just open your Zoom app, start your Personal Meeting Room, and your student will join from the waiting room. Simple as that.

Having trouble? If you run into any issues finding your link or setting up Zoom, email us at support@talkcolleges.com and we'll help you get set up.

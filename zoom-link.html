How to Get Your Personal Zoom Link

📅 June 2026 ⏱ 3 min read 👤 TalkColleges Team

When you sign up as a TalkColleges advisor, we ask for your personal Zoom link so that students can join your sessions directly. If you've never used Zoom before — or just aren't sure where to find your link — this guide walks you through it in under five minutes.

What is a Personal Zoom Link?

Zoom gives every account a Personal Meeting Room — a permanent virtual space with a fixed link that never changes. Think of it like a permanent address for your video calls. When a student books a session with you, we'll send them this link in their confirmation email so they can join at the scheduled time.

Free account is fine. You don't need a paid Zoom plan to use TalkColleges. A free Zoom account gives you everything you need — just be aware that free accounts limit group calls to 40 minutes, but one-on-one sessions have no time limit.

Step 1 — Create a Free Zoom Account

If you don't already have Zoom, go to zoom.us/signup and create a free account using your email address. It takes about two minutes.

Step 2 — Find Your Personal Meeting Link

Once you have an account, here's how to find your personal link:

1
Go to zoom.us and sign in

Head to zoom.us in your browser and click Sign In in the top right corner.

2
Open your Profile

In the left sidebar, click Profile. This is where all your account settings live.

3
Scroll down to "Personal Meeting ID"

You'll see a section labeled Personal Meeting ID with a number and a link next to it — something like https://zoom.us/j/1234567890.

4
Copy the link

Click Copy the invitation or just copy the URL directly. That's your personal Zoom link — paste it into the TalkColleges advisor form.

Using the Zoom Desktop App Instead?

If you prefer the app, open Zoom, click the Home tab, then click New Meeting and look for the Copy Invitation button. Your personal meeting link will be in the copied text.

Tip: Make sure your Personal Meeting Room has a waiting room enabled so you can admit students at the right time. Go to Settings → In Meeting (Advanced) and toggle on Waiting Room.

What Happens Next?

Once you submit your Zoom link to TalkColleges, we store it securely on your advisor profile. When a student books a session with you and completes payment, we automatically send them your Zoom link via email — you don't have to do anything extra.

On the day of the session, just open your Zoom app, start your Personal Meeting Room, and your student will join from the waiting room. Simple as that.

Having trouble? If you run into any issues finding your link or setting up Zoom, email us at support@talkcolleges.com and we'll help you get set up.
